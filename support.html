Contact us

Email osmancemgoken@gmail.com with the game name, your device (e.g. iPhone 14, Android tablet) and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 business days, in English or Turkish.

Frequently asked questions

I bought "Remove Ads" or coins but they are missing.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, then use Restore Purchases in the game's Settings or store screen. If it still doesn't work, email us your purchase receipt and we will help.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone/iPad at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android via Google Play > Order history.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it. If you changed devices, let us know and we will try to help.

The game crashes or a button doesn't work.

Please update to the latest version and restart your device. If the problem continues, email us what you tapped just before it happened.

What data do the games collect? How can I delete it?

See our Privacy Policy. To request deletion of any data linked to you, email us and we will handle it within 30 days.
